## Known limitations / not claimed

  fallback (skipped evidence NOT_ASSERTED, baseline INCOMPLETE).
- `ObservationEnd` carries no retryability field (none invented).
- The registry grows per task id for the Client's lifetime (unbounded); no local deadline on unary waits beyond the transport's.
- After an uncertain view, callback `TaskUpdateEvent.getTask()` is the last safely interpreted task.
